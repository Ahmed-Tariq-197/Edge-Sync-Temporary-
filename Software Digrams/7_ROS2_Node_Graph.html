📋 Complete DDS Quality of Service (QoS) Specification
ROS 2 Topic Message Type Reliability Durability History / Depth Design Rationale
/edgesync/handoff_request HandoffRequest.msg RELIABLE VOLATILE Keep Last, 10 Initiates handoff; carries 512-dim embedding and 3D kinematics. Delivery must be guaranteed.
/edgesync/handoff_response HandoffResponse.msg RELIABLE VOLATILE Keep Last, 10 Critical accept/reject commitment. FastDDS retransmission applies if packet dropped.
/edgesync/handoff_cancel HandoffCancel.msg RELIABLE VOLATILE Keep Last, 10 Explicit transaction termination (watchdog, timeout, Re-ID mismatch). Enforces single-owner invariant.
/edgesync/acq_status AcqStatus.msg BEST_EFFORT VOLATILE Keep Last, 1 (Deadline 150ms, Lease 300ms) 10 Hz liveness heartbeat. Retransmission is unwanted; 3 missed heartbeats trip anti-deadlock watchdog.
/edgesync/handoff_confirm HandoffConfirm.msg RELIABLE VOLATILE Keep Last, 10 Receiver signals successful acquisition and Re-ID match. Carries current epoch e.
/edgesync/handoff_confirm_ack HandoffConfirmAck.msg RELIABLE VOLATILE Keep Last, 10 Previous owner releases exclusive ownership and activates monotonic new_epoch e+1 (FR-54).
/edgesync/state_heartbeat StateHeartbeat.msg BEST_EFFORT VOLATILE Keep Last, 1 1 Hz low-rate peer availability & resource telemetry (CPU, battery, nav state).
