6. UML Deployment Diagram

Physical Hardware & Process-Level Architecture · SAD v2.3, SRS v1.3 & Experimental Protocol v1.1

NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano (8 GB) Resident OSNet Re-ID in Unified Memory Autonomous Platform Safe-Stop (NFR-14) Tier 3 Independent Ground Truth (NFR-13)

🧠 Jetson Orin Nano (8 GB)

6-core Arm Cortex-A78AE @ 1.5GHz · 1024-core Ampere GPU · Unified LPDDR5 8GB (shared CPU/GPU) · Ubuntu 22.04 LTS · ROS 2 Humble · nvpmodel 15W mode.

👁️ Perception & Sensors

RGB-D Astra Pro Plus (RGB primary, registered depth) · RPLiDAR 2D for shared-frame map localization · MPU6050 IMU + Hall encoders via STM32F407 (FreeRTOS).

📶 Network & Middleware

5GHz Wi-Fi · FastDDS / Cyclone DDS · PTP (IEEE 1588v2) time synchronization · Binary CDR serialization (≈ 2.5–3 KB max packet).

🎯 Independent Ground Truth

Time-synchronized overhead optical motion-capture rig with passive fiducials (SRS NFR-13). Evaluated offline via C-14 without online influence.
