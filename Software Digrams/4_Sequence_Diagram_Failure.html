🚨 Scenario A: Receiver Crash & 10Hz Anti-Deadlock Watchdog

Demonstrates how the 10 Hz ACQ_STATUS heartbeat aborts a doomed transaction in ≈ 300 ms (saving > 400 ms over waiting for the 700 ms acquisition timeout) when the receiver experiences power/compute failure.

        deactivate C06A
        C09A ->> C11A: Log: HANDOFF_ABORTED (latency=300ms)
    end

    Note over C09A: Bounded Recovery Phase (FR-56)
    rect rgb(30, 27, 75)
        C09A ->> C09A: State: COMMITTED to RECOVERY
        alt Remaining T_TTO safe >= 1.23s AND Rank-2 exists
            C09A ->> C09A: State: RECOVERY to HANDOFF_CANDIDATE
        else Two-UGV baseline: No other robot available
            C09A ->> C09A: State: RECOVERY to TRACKING (Owner Retained)
        else T_TTO safe < 1.23s (Zone 3 reached)
            C09A ->> C09A: State: RECOVERY to TERMINAL_FAILURE
        end
        deactivate C09A
    end
