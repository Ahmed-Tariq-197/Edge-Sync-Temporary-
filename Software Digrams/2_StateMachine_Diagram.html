2. State Machine Diagram (FSM)

EdgeSync Dual-Robot Finite State Machine · SAD v2.3, SRS v1.3 & Math v2.3 Aligned Baseline

Zone 1/2/3 Operational Regimes ≤ 700ms Hard Acquisition Timeout 10Hz Anti-Deadlock Watchdog (300ms) Monotonic Epoch e+1 Handshake

🛡️ Invariant 1: Single Ownership & Standby Handshake

Acceptance (COMMITTED) never transfers ownership. Robot A remains sole exclusive tracking owner until explicit HANDOFF_CONFIRM_ACK completes and transitions to STANDBY only upon receiving telemetry indicating Robot B is active OWNER at epoch e+1. If ACK is lost, Robot B aborts and Robot A reclaims target after T_claim (FR-47, FR-54).

⏱️ Invariant 2: Bounded Time Budget

Budget: Δt_exec^max = 866ms, Δt_safety = 364ms → Δt_budget^max = 1230ms (1.23s). Acquisition hard cutoff is ≤ 700ms (starts at ACCEPT). Zone 3 (< 1.23s) strictly forbids new transactions (Math v2.3 §3.2, §7).

🚨 Invariant 3: Anti-Deadlock Liveness Watchdog

During ACQUISITION and IDENTITY_VERIFICATION, receiver streams ACQ_STATUS at 10 Hz. Three consecutive missed heartbeats (300ms) trigger ERR_CANDIDATE_DEAD abort, preventing death lock and saving ≤400ms vs full cutoff (FR-55).
