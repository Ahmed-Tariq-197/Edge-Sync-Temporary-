2. State Machine Diagram (FSM)

EdgeSync Dual-Robot Finite State Machine · SAD v2.3, SRS v1.3 & Math v2.3 Aligned Baseline

Zone 1/2/3 Operational Regimes ≤ 700ms Hard Acquisition Timeout 10Hz Anti-Deadlock Watchdog (300ms) Monotonic Epoch e+1 Handshake

🛡️ Invariant 1: Single Ownership

Acceptance (COMMITTED) does never transfer ownership. Robot A remains sole exclusive tracking owner until explicit HANDOFF_CONFIRM_ACK completes. No duplicate ownership permitted (FR-47, FR-54).

⏱️ Invariant 2: Bounded Time Budget

Budget: Δt_exec^max = 866ms, Δt_safety = 364ms → Δt_budget^max = 1230ms (1.23s). Acquisition hard cutoff is ≤ 700ms. Zone 3 (< 1.23s) strictly forbids new transactions (Math v2.3 §3.2).

🚨 Invariant 3: Anti-Deadlock Watchdog

During acquisition, receiver streams ACQ_STATUS at 10 Hz. Three consecutive missed heartbeats (τ_hb = 300ms) trigger ERR_CANDIDATE_DEAD abort, saving >400ms compared to waiting for the full timeout (FR-55).
